Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · iPhone and Apple Watch app

Mindharbor does not collect, upload or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Support

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app? Email asafan84@gmail.com and include your device and the app language. You'll get a reply within a few days.

What stays on your device

To show your progress and remember your choices, the app keeps a small amount of information on your iPhone and Apple Watch only:

  • Your practice history: the date, length and type of each meditation, used for the Journey screen and your streak.
  • Your settings: language, voice, sound volumes and reminder time.
  • On Apple Watch: the minutes you practiced today.

This information never leaves your device through Mindharbor, and it is deleted when you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud, Apple may include it in that backup under your own Apple settings.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your iPhone itself. We do not send push notifications and do not receive any information about them.

Sound, voices and music

All guidance voices, music and nature sounds are built into the app. Mindharbor does not use the internet, and it does not access your microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or Health data.

Apple Watch

The watch app uses gentle taps and short spoken cues, and asks watchOS to keep a mindfulness session running while you practice. It does not read or write any health data.

Third parties

Mindharbor contains no third-party code that collects data. Apple may share anonymous, combined App Store statistics with the developer, such as download counts and crash reports you chose to share, as described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Mindharbor does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated. For questions, email the developer, Asaf Anselmi, at asafan84@gmail.com.

  • ההגדרות שלכם: שפה, קול, עוצמות צליל ושעת התזכורת.
  • בשעון: מספר הדקות שתרגלתם היום.

המידע הזה לא יוצא מהמכשיר דרך Mindharbor, והוא נמחק כשמוחקים את האפליקציה. אם אתם מגבים את האייפון ל-iCloud, אפל עשויה לכלול אותו בגיבוי לפי ההגדרות שלכם.
